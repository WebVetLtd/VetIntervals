VetIntervals Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Last Updated: June 2026

Privacy Policy

Overview

VetIntervals is designed to operate as a local veterinary interval and date reference tool.

Information Stored

You may choose to enter information such as:

  • Animal names
  • Vaccination dates
  • Treatment dates
  • Competition dates
  • Notes
  • Reminder dates
  • Saved calculations

This information is stored locally on your device to provide app functionality.

Data Collection

VetIntervals does not require account creation or user registration.

VetIntervals does not intentionally collect, transmit, store, or process your entered data on servers operated by WebVet Ltd.

Data Sharing

VetIntervals does not sell personal information.

Information entered into the app is not intentionally shared with the app developer or third parties.

Calendar Access

If you choose to create reminders or calendar events, VetIntervals may request permission to access your device calendar.

Any calendar entries created by the app are managed by your device operating system and your chosen calendar provider.

Permissions

VetIntervals may request access to device features such as the calendar where required to create reminders or calendar entries.

You may grant or revoke permissions at any time through your device settings.

Device Storage

Saved calculations, settings, and acceptance of this policy may be stored locally on your device.

Deleting the app, clearing app data, or resetting your device may permanently remove this information.

Data Retention

Information entered into VetIntervals remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the application.

WebVet Ltd does not maintain a server-side copy of your entered data and cannot recover information deleted from your device.

Children's Privacy

VetIntervals is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
