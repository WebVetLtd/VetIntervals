VetIntervals Privacy Policy & Disclaimer

Last Updated: June 2026

Privacy Policy

VetIntervals is designed to operate as a local veterinary interval and date reference tool.

Information Stored

  • Animal names
  • Vaccination dates
  • Treatment dates
  • Competition dates
  • Notes
  • Reminder dates
  • Saved calculations

This information is stored locally on your device to provide app functionality.

Data Collection

VetIntervals does not require user registration or account creation.

VetIntervals does not intentionally collect, transmit, store, or process your entered data on servers operated by WebVet Ltd.

Data Sharing

VetIntervals does not sell personal information.

Information entered into the app is not intentionally shared with the app developer or third parties.

Disclaimer

VetIntervals is provided as an informational and assistive tool only.

The app does not provide veterinary, medical, legal, regulatory, or professional advice.

Use of the app does not guarantee vaccination compliance, competition eligibility, regulatory compliance, veterinary compliance, or legal compliance.

Users are responsible for independently verifying all requirements with the relevant veterinary surgeon, governing body, competition organiser, regulator, or other appropriate authority.
